Jeric Raval

About Jeric Raval

Ricardo Baluyut Buensuceso, known professionally as Jeric Raval, is a Filipino film and television actor who became widely recognized for his action and antagonist roles. He rose to prominence during the 1990s, when Philippine cinema produced numerous action films centered on crime, revenge, law enforcement, and street conflicts.

Raval initially explored the possibility of entering the entertainment industry through music. However, he was encouraged to pursue acting instead. He made his film debut in Primitivo ’Ebok’ Ala in 1992, beginning a career that would make him a familiar face in Filipino action cinema.

During the 1990s, he appeared in numerous action films, often portraying criminals, gang leaders, enemies of the protagonist, and other physically demanding characters. His screen presence and ability to portray intimidating figures helped establish him as a recognizable action performer. He became particularly associated with films that featured confrontations, gunfights, revenge plots, and crime-related stories.

Raval later expanded his work into television. He appeared in productions such as FPJ’s Ang Probinsyano, The Blood Sisters, and other drama series, frequently taking roles that drew on his established tough-guy image. His experience in action cinema also made him a recognizable performer in supporting and antagonist roles.

He is also the father of actress AJ Raval, among his children. His family has attracted public attention because of its connections to Philippine entertainment.

Raval’s contribution to action cinema lies in his extensive work during the genre’s commercially active years. He became one of the performers audiences associated with the tough, uncompromising characters that defined many Filipino action films of the period.
