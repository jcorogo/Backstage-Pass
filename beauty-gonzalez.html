About Beauty Gonzalez

Beauty Gonzalez, born Beauty Kelly Roxanne Gonzalez on May 28, 1991, in Dumaguete City, is a Filipino actress and former reality television personality. She first gained public attention as a housemate on Pinoy Big Brother: Teen Edition Plus in 2008 before developing a career in acting.

Gonzalez entered show business after her reality television appearance and began receiving opportunities in film and television. Her early film work included My Only U and Sa ’yo Lamang. She later appeared in productions such as Tuhog, Starting Over Again, and The Achy Breaky Hearts.

Her horror and thriller work includes Feng Shui 2 (2014), where she appeared as Ellen, and Hellcome Home (2019), a supernatural horror film about a family confronting frightening events after moving into a house with a dark history.

She continued exploring the genre through Huwag Kang Lalabas (2021), Kampon (2023), and In My Mother’s Skin (2023). In In My Mother’s Skin, she portrayed Ligaya, a mother living during World War II whose family becomes entangled in a disturbing supernatural situation involving a mysterious fairy.

Her performance in In My Mother’s Skin attracted international attention because of the film’s combination of historical drama, body horror, and Filipino folklore. The production became one of the notable Filipino genre films to reach international streaming audiences.

Gonzalez’s other television and film projects include Kadenang Ginto, Ika-6 na Utos, Nag-aapoy na Damdamin, Gensan Punch, Outside, and The Time That Remains. Her career has expanded beyond romantic and family drama into psychological and supernatural storytelling.
