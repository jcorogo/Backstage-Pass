Jose Manalo

About Jose Manalo

Ariel Pagtalonia Manalo, professionally known as Jose Manalo, is a Filipino comedian, actor, television host, and performer. He became widely recognized through his work on Eat Bulaga! and his long-running comedic partnership with Wally Bayola and Paolo Ballesteros.

Before becoming a familiar television personality, Manalo worked in several behind-the-scenes positions. He initially entered the entertainment industry through production work, serving as a production assistant, audition master, and floor director. He also performed as a clown and entertainer before gaining opportunities to appear on camera.

His natural comedic ability eventually brought him into the spotlight. He became a regular performer on Eat Bulaga! and developed a strong audience following through improvisation, character acting, impersonations, and spontaneous exchanges with other hosts.

His partnership with Wally Bayola became one of the most recognizable comedy tandems on Philippine television. Their chemistry developed through hosting assignments and eventually became a central part of their work in the show’s public-service segments. Together with Paolo Ballesteros, they became known as JoWaPao.

Manalo also appeared in films, including projects in the Enteng Kabisote series and Ispiritista: Itay, May Moomoo!. His television credits include comedy and variety programs that allowed him to develop characters beyond his noontime-show persona.

He became especially popular through the Juan for All, All for Juan segment, where he and his fellow hosts interacted with ordinary Filipinos in communities around the country. The segment combined comedy, public service, and spontaneous entertainment.

Manalo’s career is an example of a performer who progressed from backstage production work into mainstream television stardom. His comedic partnership with Wally Bayola remains one of the most recognizable duos in Filipino variety television.
