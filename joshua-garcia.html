About Joshua Garcia

Joshua Garcia, born Joshua Espineli Garcia on October 7, 1997, in Bauan, Batangas, is a Filipino actor who became known for his emotionally expressive performances and ability to portray relatable young men in romantic and dramatic stories.

Garcia first gained national attention as a housemate in Pinoy Big Brother: All In in 2014. His participation introduced him to viewers and helped him secure opportunities through Star Magic. He began acting professionally soon afterward.

His major breakthrough came through the film Vince & Kath & James (2016), where he portrayed the character James. The film became a commercial success and earned him recognition as a promising young actor.

He followed it with Love You to the Stars and Back (2017), opposite Julia Barretto. Their onscreen chemistry became popular, leading to additional projects such as Unexpectedly Yours and the television series Ngayon at Kailanman.

Garcia also demonstrated his dramatic range in The Good Son (2017–2018), where he portrayed a young man caught in a complicated family conflict. He later appeared in The Killer Bride, Viral, and Darna, expanding his television work into suspense and fantasy.

His film credits include I Love You, Hater, Block Z, Ang Mga Kaibigan ni Mama Susan, Un/Happy for You, and Meet, Greet & Bye. His performance in Un/Happy for You opposite Julia Barretto renewed public interest in their onscreen partnership and became a major box-office success.

Garcia’s more recent television work includes It’s Okay to Not Be Okay, the Philippine adaptation of the Korean drama, in which he plays a leading role alongside Anne Curtis and Carlo Aquino.

He has received recognition from Philippine award-giving organizations, including awards for his television and film performances. His acting style is often associated with natural emotional delivery and an ability to portray vulnerability.

Garcia’s career is notable for his transformation from a reality-show housemate into one of the prominent dramatic and romantic leading men of his generation.

Joshua Espineli Garcia was born on October 7, 1997, in Bauan, Batangas. His childhood was shaped by difficult family circumstances, and he was raised for a significant period by his paternal relatives, including his uncle, a priest, and his grandmother. His experiences growing up helped shape the determination he later brought to his acting career.

Garcia entered the public eye in 2014 as a housemate on Pinoy Big Brother: All In. Although he did not reach the final Big Four, his appearance on the program opened the door to a career with Star Magic. He began acting in television dramas, including Nasaan Ka Nang Kailangan Kita, before gaining wider recognition through his performances in The Greatest Love and The Good Son.

His film breakthrough came with Vince and Kath and James, followed by Love You to the Stars and Back, both of which helped establish him as a promising young leading man. His on-screen pairing with Julia Barretto became known as JoshLia, and the two appeared in projects including Unexpectedly Yours, I Love You, Hater, and Ngayon at Kailanman. Garcia later demonstrated his ability to take on more mature roles in The Killer Bride, Unbreak My Heart, and Un/Happy For You. In 2024, he renewed his contract with ABS-CBN, reaffirming his relationship with the network that launched his career.
