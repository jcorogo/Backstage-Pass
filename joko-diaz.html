About Joko Diaz

Joko Diaz, born on April 26, 1976, is a Filipino actor known for his action roles, crime dramas, and portrayals of tough or antagonistic characters. He comes from a family with a long history in Philippine cinema and began appearing in films as a child.

Diaz entered the film industry at a young age, appearing in productions during the 1980s. His early credits include Boy Negro, Kumander Bawang: Kalaban ng mga Aswang, and Sigaw ng Katarungan. These early projects gave him experience in action, adventure, and stories involving supernatural folklore.

As an adult performer, he became known for action films and crime dramas, frequently portraying gangsters, police officers, and dangerous figures. His filmography includes Mistah, The Grepor Butch Belgica Story, Manila Kingpin: The Asiong Salonga Story, El Presidente, and Manila’s Finest.

His horror-related and supernatural work includes early productions involving aswang mythology and later projects such as Mga Mata sa Dilim. He has also appeared in television dramas that include suspense, mystery, and crime elements.

Diaz’s television work includes FPJ’s Ang Probinsyano, Kadenang Ginto, Marry Me, Marry You, Mars Ravelo’s Darna, Flower of Evil, Pamilya Sagrado, and Sins of the Father. These productions have demonstrated his ability to play both threatening antagonists and supporting dramatic characters.

His long career is notable for his physical screen presence and familiarity with action-oriented storytelling. Although he is primarily associated with action and crime, his early involvement in supernatural and folklore-based productions also connects him to Filipino horror cinema.

Diaz remains a recognizable character actor whose filmography reflects several decades of Philippine action and genre filmmaking.
