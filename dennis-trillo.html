About Dennis Trillo

Dennis Trillo, born Abelardo Dennis Florencio Ho on May 12, 1981, in Quezon City, is a Filipino actor, singer, and television personality. He is widely recognized for his dramatic versatility and his ability to portray complex, morally difficult characters.

Trillo began his entertainment career in the late 1990s and became a GMA Network artist in the early 2000s. His early work included television dramas and supporting film roles. His performance in Aishite Imasu 1941: Mahal Kita (2004) brought him significant critical attention and helped establish him as a serious actor.

His horror and thriller projects include Txt (2006), Pamahiin (2006), Mag-ingat Ka sa... Kulam (2008), Tarot (2009), and Shake, Rattle & Roll XV (2014). These films gave him opportunities to portray characters confronting supernatural forces, curses, and unexplained events.

He also appeared in Sapi (2013), a psychological thriller involving possession and disturbing behavior. His role in these productions demonstrated his ability to work in stories that depend on tension, mystery, and unsettling character behavior.

Outside horror, Trillo became known for My Husband’s Lover, Temptation of Wife, Legal Wives, Maria Clara at Ibarra, and Pulang Araw. His film performances in Felix Manalo, On the Job: The Missing 8, and Green Bones further strengthened his reputation. His work in Green Bones earned him major Best Actor recognition.

Trillo has received numerous awards from Philippine film and television organizations. He is regarded as one of the country’s most accomplished actors of his generation, particularly for his ability to move between commercial entertainment and serious dramatic roles.

His horror work forms one part of a wide-ranging career that includes psychological thrillers, supernatural stories, historical drama, and crime films.
