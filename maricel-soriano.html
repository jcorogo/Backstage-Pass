About Maricel Soriano

Maria Cecilia Dador Soriano, professionally known as Maricel Soriano, is a Filipino actress and television personality widely known as the Diamond Star of Philippine cinema. Born on February 25, 1965, she began her career as a child actress and developed one of the longest and most celebrated careers in Philippine film and television.

Soriano made her film debut in My Heart Belongs to Daddy (1971). She received early recognition for her performance in Alaala Mo, Daigdig Ko (1973), earning a Best Child Actress award. During this period, she also became familiar to television audiences through the long-running sitcom John en Marsha, where she portrayed Shirley Puruntong.

During the 1980s, Soriano became one of the prominent performers associated with Regal Films and its Regal Babies. She starred in films such as Inday Bote, Batang Quiapo, Saan Darating ang Umaga?, and Pinulot Ka Lang sa Lupa. These projects helped establish her as a major leading actress.

Her career reached another level through dramatic films such as Ikaw Pa Lang ang Minahal, Separada, Dahas, Nasaan ang Puso, Soltera, Abandonada, Filipinas, and Inang Yaya. Her performances frequently involved complicated women dealing with family conflict, betrayal, sacrifice, and social pressure.

Soriano also became a major television performer through Maricel Regal Drama Special and The Maricel Drama Special. These anthology programs gave her opportunities to portray different characters and demonstrate her dramatic range.

Her later film appearances include A Love Story, Bahay Kubo: A Pinoy Mano Po!, Yesterday, Today & Tomorrow, The Heiress, In His Mother’s Eyes, and Meet, Greet & Bye. Her performance in In His Mother’s Eyes earned her a Luna Award for Best Actress in 2024.

Across her career, Soriano has received numerous awards, including multiple FAMAS, Luna, and television acting honors. Her longevity, versatility, and ability to portray both comedic and dramatic characters have made her one of the most respected actresses in Philippine entertainment.
