About Barbie Forteza

Barbie Forteza is a Filipino actress, singer, and television host. Born Barbara Ambas Forteza on July 31, 1997, in Biñan, Laguna, she began her career as a child actress and gradually became one of GMA Network’s most recognizable leading women.

Forteza entered show business in 2009 when she portrayed young Jodi in the Philippine adaptation of the Korean drama Stairway to Heaven. Her performance attracted attention, and she soon received more substantial roles.

Her early television projects included First Time, Pilyang Kerubin, and Tween Hearts. The latter became particularly important in establishing her popularity among younger audiences. She subsequently received leading roles in Ikaw Lang ang Mamahalin, Luna Blanca, and The Half Sisters.

Her career continued to develop through romantic and dramatic projects such as Meant to Be, Inday Will Always Love You, Kara Mia, and Anak ni Waray vs. Anak ni Biday. These roles allowed her to portray different types of young women and demonstrate her versatility.

One of her most acclaimed performances came in Maria Clara at Ibarra (2022–2023), where she portrayed Klay, a modern-day woman transported into the world of José Rizal’s novels. Her portrayal required her to balance contemporary humor with historical and literary drama.

She later appeared in Pulang Araw (2024), a historical series set during the Japanese occupation. The production gave her another opportunity to take on a demanding dramatic role.

Forteza has also starred in films such as Laut, Tuos, This Time I’ll Be Sweeter, Meant to Beh, and The Missing. Her performance in independent cinema has earned recognition beyond mainstream television.

In 2025, she appeared in Beauty Empire, while in 2026 she discussed her transition toward more mature roles and confirmed that she had no plans to leave show business. Her career is notable for its longevity, versatility, and successful transition from child actress to leading performer.
