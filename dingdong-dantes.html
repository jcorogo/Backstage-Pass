About Dingdong Dantes

Dingdong Dantes, born Jose Sixto Raphael Gonzalez Dantes III on August 2, 1980, is a Filipino actor, television host, producer, and entrepreneur. He is one of the most established leading men in Philippine entertainment and has worked in television and film since childhood.

Dantes began his career as a child performer, appearing in commercials and film projects. He later became part of the youth-oriented group Abztract Dancers and continued developing his skills in acting and television performance.

His career expanded through television dramas and fantasy productions. He became widely recognized through T.G.I.S. and later starred in Mulawin (2004), where he portrayed Ybrahim. The fantasy series became a major success and established him as one of GMA Network’s leading actors.

Dantes continued with television productions such as Encantadia, Marimar, Dyesebel, Stairway to Heaven, Endless Love, Genesis, Carmela, and Alyas Robin Hood. His roles often combined romance, action, fantasy, and family drama.

His film work includes Moments of Love, One True Love, Segunda Mano, She’s the One, The Unmarried Wife, and The Dingdong Dantes Story. He also starred opposite Marian Rivera in numerous productions, helping establish one of Philippine entertainment’s best-known celebrity partnerships.

One of his most significant recent film projects was Rewind (2023), in which he starred with Marian Rivera. The film became a historic box-office success and helped renew interest in theatrical moviegoing. His other recent work includes Firefly as a producer and various television hosting projects.

Dantes is also a familiar television host through programs such as Family Feud Philippines, Amazing Earth, and The Voice Kids. He has received numerous awards for acting and hosting, including major recognition from Philippine television and film organizations.

Outside entertainment, Dantes has been involved in public service, youth development, and advocacy. He has also served in the Philippine Navy Reserve and founded initiatives supporting Filipino filmmakers and performers.

His career is distinguished by its longevity and versatility. From child performer to fantasy-series star, film actor, and television host, Dantes has maintained a prominent place in Philippine entertainment.
