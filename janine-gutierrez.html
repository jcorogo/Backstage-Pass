About Janine Gutierrez

Janine Marie Elizabeth de Leon Gutierrez was born on October 2, 1989. She comes from a prominent family of Filipino entertainers: her parents are actors Ramon Christopher Gutierrez and Lotlot de Leon, while her grandparents include actor Eddie Gutierrez, singer Pilita Corrales, actor Christopher de Leon, and actress Nora Aunor. Despite her family background, she pursued her education before entering show business and graduated from Ateneo de Manila University with a degree in European Studies.

Gutierrez began her professional career with GMA Network in 2011. Her early television appearances included Party Pilipinas, Legacy, and Villa Quintana. She gradually developed into a leading actress through dramas such as The Love Affair, Legally Blind, Victor Magtanggol, and Dragon Lady. She also gained critical recognition through independent films, particularly Babae at Baril, for which she received the Best Actress award at the QCinema International Film Festival.

In 2021, Gutierrez moved to ABS-CBN, expanding her opportunities in television and film. Her later projects include Marry Me, Marry You, Dirty Linen, Linlang, Lavender Fields, and The Loyalty Game. Her work has demonstrated a range that extends from romantic drama to psychological and socially grounded storytelling. In 2026, she marked her fifteenth year in show business and signed with Star Magic, entering another stage of her career.
