About Julia Montes

Mara Hautea Schnittka, professionally known as Julia Montes, is a Filipino actress recognized for her dramatic performances in television series and films. Born on March 19, 1995, in Pandacan, Manila, she began her entertainment career as a child performer and gradually became one of the leading dramatic actresses of her generation.

Montes first appeared in television commercials and children’s programs. She gained early experience through supporting roles in television productions, including Goin’ Bulilit. These appearances helped her develop the acting skills that would later become central to her career.

Her breakthrough came with the 2010 remake of Mara Clara, where she portrayed Clara del Valle opposite Kathryn Bernardo as Mara. Her performance as the antagonist earned attention for its emotional intensity and helped establish her as a serious dramatic actress.

She followed this success with Walang Hanggan, Muling Buksan ang Puso, Ikaw Lamang, and Doble Kara. In Doble Kara, she played identical twins Kara and Sara, a demanding role that required her to create two distinct characters with different personalities and experiences.

Montes also starred in Asintado, 24/7, and All of Me. Her film work includes The Reunion, A Moment in Time, Halik sa Hangin, Padre de Familia, Five Breakups and a Romance, and Topakk.

One of her later television projects was Saving Grace, the Filipino adaptation of the Japanese drama Mother. The production gave her an emotionally demanding role involving a child in danger and the complicated decisions of an adult who tries to protect her.

Montes has received recognition from Philippine award-giving organizations for her work in television drama. She is particularly known for portraying women facing difficult family situations, emotional trauma, and moral conflicts.

Her career is notable for the strength of her dramatic performances and her progression from child actress to leading television performer.
