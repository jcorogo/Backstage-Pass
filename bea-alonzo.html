About Bea Alonzo

Phylbert Angellie Ranollo Fagestrom, professionally known as Bea Alonzo, is a Filipino actress and businesswoman recognized for her performances in romantic dramas, family films, television melodramas, and independent productions. Born on October 17, 1987, she entered show business as a teenager and became one of the country’s most prominent dramatic leading actresses.

Alonzo began her career in the early 2000s after joining ABS-CBN’s Star Circle talent program. Her early television appearances included K2BU, Kay Tagal Kang Hinintay, and It Might Be You. These projects gave her opportunities to work alongside established performers and gradually develop her dramatic abilities.

Her film career grew through projects such as My First Romance, Now That I Have You, and Close to You. Her onscreen partnership with John Lloyd Cruz became one of the most successful pairings in Philippine cinema. Together, they starred in One More Chance (2007), Miss You Like Crazy (2010), and A Second Chance (2015). Her portrayal of Basha in One More Chance became one of her most recognizable performances.

Alonzo also demonstrated her dramatic range in films such as The Mistress, The Love Affair, The Second Woman, Eerie, and Unbreakable. Her work in Four Sisters and a Wedding (2013), where she portrayed Bobbie Salazar, became particularly memorable for its portrayal of family tensions, sibling relationships, and personal expectations.

Her television career includes Maging Sino Ka Man, Magkaribal, A Beautiful Affair, Sana Bukas pa ang Kahapon, and A Love to Last. She later moved to GMA Network, where she starred in Start-Up PH and Widow’s War.

Alonzo has received numerous awards and nominations for her acting. Beyond entertainment, she has pursued business interests and developed projects outside the traditional film and television industry.

Her career is distinguished by her ability to portray emotionally complicated women, particularly characters dealing with love, family conflict, betrayal, and personal independence.
