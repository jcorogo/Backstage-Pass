About Angel Locsin

Angelica Locsin Colmenares, professionally known as Angel Locsin, is a Filipino actress, humanitarian, and advocate. Born on April 23, 1985, in Santa Maria, Bulacan, she became one of the country’s most recognizable actresses through her portrayals of heroines, strong female characters, and emotionally complex dramatic roles.

Locsin began her entertainment career in the early 2000s after gaining opportunities in television and modeling. Her early work included supporting appearances before she received more prominent roles.

Her breakthrough came when she portrayed Alwina in GMA Network’s fantasy series Mulawin (2004). The program became a major television success and introduced her to audiences as a leading actress. She followed this with her portrayal of the Filipino superheroine in Darna (2005), which became one of the defining roles of her early career.

Locsin continued appearing in television productions such as Majika, Asian Treasures, Lobo, Only You, Impostora, and The General’s Daughter. Her performance in Lobo earned international attention and helped demonstrate her ability to portray a character balancing romance, action, and supernatural drama.

Her film career includes In the Name of Love, Unofficially Yours, One More Try, Four Sisters and a Wedding, Everything About Her, and The Third Party. Her performance in One More Try earned her major Best Actress recognition, while Everything About Her demonstrated her ability to portray a woman dealing with illness, family relationships, and personal sacrifice.

Locsin became equally recognized for her humanitarian work. She has participated in disaster-relief efforts, fundraising activities, and charitable initiatives supporting communities affected by typhoons and other emergencies. Her public service work became an important part of her identity beyond acting.

She married film producer Neil Arce in 2021. After a period away from regular acting projects, she announced her retirement from acting in 2026 and has continued to be associated with humanitarian and advocacy work.

Locsin’s legacy combines her memorable television and film performances with her commitment to helping vulnerable communities. She remains one of the most recognizable actresses and public-service personalities in contemporary Philippine entertainment.
