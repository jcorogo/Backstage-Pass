About Eugene Domingo

Eugenia Roxas Domingo, professionally known as Eugene Domingo, is a Filipino actress, comedian, and television host. Born on July 23, 1971, in Malate, Manila, she developed a career characterized by expressive character acting, intelligent comedic timing, theatrical training, and versatility across film and television.

Domingo studied at the University of the Philippines Diliman, where she developed her interest in theater and acting. She became involved in stage productions and gained professional experience through theatrical performances before building a mainstream screen career.

Her early television and film appearances included supporting roles in comedy and drama productions. She became particularly recognizable through her work with Ai-Ai delas Alas in the Ang Tanging Ina film series, where she played Rowena, one of the supporting characters.

Her major breakthrough as a leading actress came with Kimmy Dora: Kambal sa Kiyeme (2009), where she portrayed identical twins Kimmy and Dora Go Dong Hae. The film showcased her ability to create two distinct personalities and became a major success. She reprised the characters in Kimmy Dora and the Temple of Kiyeme and Kimmy Dora: Ang Kiyemeng Prequel.

Another defining project was Ang Babae sa Septic Tank (2011), a satirical film that examined the Philippine independent-film industry and its conventions. Her portrayal of a self-important actress navigating a filmmaking project earned significant critical recognition. She also appeared in Here Comes the Bride, Mamarazzi, Working Girls, and The Panti Sisters.

Domingo’s television career includes Bubble Gang, Celebrity Bluff, Dear Uge, and other comedy programs. She is also known for her theatrical performances, which have demonstrated her ability to move between stage comedy and dramatic acting.

Her career has earned numerous awards and nominations, making her one of the most respected comedy actresses in the Philippines. Her versatility and commitment to character-driven performances have made her an important figure in contemporary Filipino cinema.
