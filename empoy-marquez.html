Empoy Marquez

About Empoy Marquez

Julius Erman Ramos Marquez, professionally known as Empoy Marquez, is a Filipino actor, comedian, and television host. He became widely recognized for his natural comedic delivery, expressive facial reactions, understated humor, and ability to portray ordinary people in amusing or emotionally relatable situations.

Marquez entered the entertainment industry after winning the Mr. Suave look-alike contest on ABS-CBN’s Magandang Tanghali Bayan in 2003. The competition earned him an acting opportunity in the comedy film Mr. Suave, which became his introduction to mainstream cinema.

He gradually built his career through supporting roles in films and television. His credits include Ang Tanging Ina, Hating Kapatid, My Illegal Wife, Da Possessed, and Bride for Rent. He became a familiar performer in comedy productions, often playing characters whose humor came from their personality, expressions, and everyday struggles.

His biggest career breakthrough came with Kita Kita (2017), in which he starred opposite Alessandra de Rossi. He portrayed Tonyo, a Filipino tour guide in Japan who develops a friendship and romantic connection with a woman who temporarily loses her sight. The film became a major commercial success and one of the most successful Filipino independent films of its time.

His performance received critical recognition and a Gawad Urian Best Actor nomination. The film also helped audiences recognize his potential as a leading actor rather than simply a supporting comedian.

Marquez continued appearing in films such as Kusina Kings, Hayop Ka!, Walang KaParis, Kidnap for Romance, My Zombabe, and Fruitcake. His television credits include Super Inggo, FPJ’s Ang Probinsyano, Niña Niño, and Black Rider.

His career is particularly notable for the success of Kita Kita, which demonstrated that a comedian known for supporting roles could successfully carry a romantic feature film.
