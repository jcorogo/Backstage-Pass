About Ai-Ai delas Alas

Martina Eileen Hernandez delas Alas, professionally known as Ai-Ai delas Alas, is a Filipino actress, comedian, singer, and television personality widely referred to as the "Comedy Concert Queen" and one of the major comedy stars of Philippine cinema.

Delas Alas was born on November 11, 1964, in San Luis, Batangas. She entered entertainment through stage performances, comedy, and television appearances. Before becoming a major film star, she spent years developing her comedic abilities through live performances and supporting roles.

Her breakthrough came through the film Ang Tanging Ina (2003), in which she portrayed Ina Montecillo, a mother struggling to raise her large family. The film became a major box-office success and established her as one of the country’s most recognizable comedy actresses.

She reprised the role in Ang Tanging Ina N’yong Lahat (2008), Ang Tanging Ina Mo: Last na ’To! (2010), and Enteng ng Ina Mo (2011). The character became one of the defining roles of her career and helped create a successful comedy-film franchise.

Delas Alas also appeared in films such as Pasukob, D’ Anothers, Tanging Yaman, Volta, Our Mighty Yaya, and Feelennial. She demonstrated her ability to work in both comedy and drama, earning recognition for performances that combined humor with emotional storytelling.

Her television career includes comedy and variety programs, including Bubble Gang, That’s My Doc, and other entertainment shows. She also pursued a recording career and released music associated with novelty and comedy entertainment.

Delas Alas has received numerous awards for her film and television performances, including major comedy and acting honors. Her influence on Philippine cinema is particularly strong because of her success in leading comedy films and her ability to portray relatable Filipino family experiences.

Her legacy rests on her status as one of the country’s most successful female comedy-film stars.
