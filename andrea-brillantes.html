About Andrea Brillantes

Andrea Brillantes is the professional name of Ramona Blythe Daguio Gorostiza, a Filipino actress who grew up in the entertainment industry. Born on March 12, 2003, in Taytay, Rizal, she began performing as a child and gradually became one of the most recognizable young actresses in Philippine television.

She started appearing in commercials and television programs before joining the children’s comedy show Goin’ Bulilit. Her early appearances gave her experience working with established actors and helped her develop her screen presence.

Her first major breakthrough came in 2013 when she played the title character in the television remake of Annaliza. Her performance as a young girl facing hardship and family difficulties introduced her as a promising dramatic actress. She followed this with appearances in Hawak Kamay, Pangako Sa ’Yo, and other television productions.

Brillantes became a household name through her portrayal of Marga Mondragon in Kadenang Ginto (2018–2020). Her character was initially presented as a spoiled and antagonistic young woman, but the role developed into a more complicated portrayal of family conflict, rivalry, and personal growth. Her performance earned her considerable attention and helped establish her as a leading young star.

She continued exploring more mature characters in Huwag Kang Mangamba, Senior High, and its continuation High Street. She also played a drag performer in the series Drag You and Me, demonstrating her willingness to take on roles outside her earlier television image.

Her film work includes The Ghosting, Ang Mga Kaibigan ni Mama Susan, and On the Job: The Missing 8. In 2025, she appeared in Rekonek, while her 2026 credits included the series A Secret in Prague. She has also explored music and digital content, including the release of her song "Tahanan."

Outside acting, Brillantes has developed business interests and become a prominent social-media personality. Her career is particularly notable because she began working at a young age and successfully transitioned from child performer to a leading actress of her generation.
