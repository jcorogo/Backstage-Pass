About Melai Cantiveros

Melisa Bunayog Cantiveros-Francisco, professionally known as Melai Cantiveros, is a Filipino comedian, actress, television host, and reality television personality. Born on April 6, 1988, in General Santos City, she became famous for her natural humor, expressive personality, and relatable approach to comedy.

Cantiveros first gained nationwide attention when she joined Pinoy Big Brother: Double Up in 2009. Her cheerful personality, spontaneous remarks, and humorous interactions with fellow housemates made her one of the season’s most memorable contestants. She eventually won the competition, opening the door to opportunities in television and entertainment.

After her reality-show victory, she joined ABS-CBN’s Star Magic and began appearing in television programs and comedy productions. Her acting projects include Precious Hearts Romances Presents: Impostor, Mara Clara, We Will Survive, Luv U, and Maalaala Mo Kaya. She also appeared in films such as The Reunion, The Gifted, and Beauty and the Bestie.

A significant achievement in her career came when she won the first season of Your Face Sounds Familiar Philippines in 2015. The competition showcased her ability to imitate singers and performers through voice, movement, costume, and character transformation. It also demonstrated that her comedic talent extended to musical performance.

Cantiveros became one of the hosts of the morning talk show Magandang Buhay, where she worked alongside other television personalities. Her hosting style is characterized by humor, warmth, spontaneity, and an approachable personality.

She has also worked with her husband, Jason Francisco, in entertainment projects and family-oriented content. Their relationship and humorous interactions have become familiar to television and online audiences.

Cantiveros’s career is notable for her transition from reality television winner to comedian, actress, and long-running television host. Her natural humor and relatable personality remain central to her public identity.
