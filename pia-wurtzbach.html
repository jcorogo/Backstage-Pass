About Pia Wurtzbach

Pia Alonzo Wurtzbach-Jauncey, professionally known as Pia Wurtzbach, is a Filipino-German actress, model, television personality, beauty queen, and advocate. Born on September 24, 1989, in Stuttgart, Germany, she grew up in the Philippines, particularly in Cagayan de Oro, before developing a career in entertainment and international pageantry.

Wurtzbach began working in entertainment as a child under the screen name Pia Romero. She appeared in television programs such as K2BU, It Might Be You, Sa Piling Mo, and Bora. Her early film appearances included Kung Ako Na Lang Sana, All My Life, and All About Love. These projects gave her experience in acting long before she became internationally famous.

In addition to acting, Wurtzbach pursued interests in fashion and media. She worked as a stylist and beauty writer for the Philippine Daily Inquirer, gaining experience in the fashion and lifestyle industry.

Her international breakthrough came through beauty pageantry. After competing in Binibining Pilipinas in 2013 and 2014, she returned in 2015 and won the title of Binibining Pilipinas Universe. She represented the Philippines at Miss Universe 2015 in Las Vegas, where she was crowned Miss Universe. Her victory became a major national celebration and made her the third Filipina to win the international title.

Following her pageant victory, Wurtzbach returned to acting and appeared in films including Gandarrapiddo: The Revenger Squad and My Perfect You. She also became a television personality and public speaker.

Her advocacy work includes HIV awareness, anti-discrimination efforts, and support for vulnerable communities. UNAIDS appointed her Goodwill Ambassador for Asia and the Pacific in 2017, recognizing her ability to use her public influence to promote awareness and inclusion.

Wurtzbach’s career combines acting, modeling, international pageantry, public advocacy, and media work. Her Miss Universe victory remains the defining achievement of her public career.
