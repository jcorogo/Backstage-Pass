About Kim Chiu

Kim Chiu, whose full name is Kimberly Sue Yap Chiu, is a Filipino actress, singer, dancer, and television host. Born on April 19, 1990, in Tacloban City, Leyte, she rose to fame after winning Pinoy Big Brother: Teen Edition in 2006.

Her television career developed through youth-oriented programs and romantic dramas. Her early breakthrough included Sana Maulit Muli, My Girl, and Tayong Dalawa. She later became a major leading actress through Ina, Kapatid, Anak, Ikaw Lamang, and Love Thy Woman.

Chiu became particularly associated with Filipino horror after starring in The Healing (2012), directed by Chito S. Roño. She portrayed a character involved in a frightening story about a faith healer whose supposed miraculous abilities bring terrifying consequences. The film gave her a challenging role involving fear and supernatural uncertainty.

She followed this with The Ghost Bride (2017), in which she portrayed Mayen, a woman who agrees to become the spiritual bride of a deceased Chinese man in an effort to help her family. The movie explores Chinese funeral traditions, family hardship, and supernatural danger.

In Da One That Ghost Away (2018), Chiu moved into horror-comedy as Carmel Monseratt, a paranormal expert. She later starred in U-Turn (2020), portraying a journalist investigating a series of mysterious deaths connected to a dangerous road ritual.

Her horror performances have earned her the popular nickname "Millennial Horror Queen" among fans and entertainment media. Her ability to portray ordinary people caught in extraordinary supernatural circumstances has become a recognizable part of her film career.

Outside horror, Chiu has starred in Bride for Rent, Bakit Hindi Ka Crush ng Crush Mo?, The Ghost Bride, One Great Love, and My Love Will Make You Disappear. She has also become a familiar host on It’s Showtime.

Chiu’s horror filmography is one of the most extensive among younger Filipino mainstream actresses, with projects covering supernatural horror, ghost stories, and horror-comedy.
