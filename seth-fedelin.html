About Seth Fedelin

Seth Fedelin is a Filipino actor and singer. Born Seth Yancy Fronda Fedelin on July 9, 2002, in Dasmariñas, Cavite, he became known after joining the reality television program Pinoy Big Brother: Otso.

Fedelin entered the entertainment industry through Pinoy Big Brother in 2018. His participation introduced him to a national audience and provided opportunities to develop his acting career through ABS-CBN.

One of his first major acting opportunities came through Kadenang Ginto, where he portrayed a character involved in the complicated relationships and conflicts surrounding the show’s central families. The series also introduced his on-screen partnership with Francine Diaz, popularly known as FranSeth.

He later appeared in Huwag Kang Mangamba, where he took on a more substantial dramatic role. His subsequent projects included Dirty Linen, Fractured, and The Iron Heart, allowing him to explore different characters and genres.

Fedelin’s film career includes My Future You (2024), in which he starred alongside Francine Diaz. The film explored romance and time-related storytelling and became an important project in his development as a leading actor. His performance received recognition at the Metro Manila Film Festival and the Manila International Film Festival.

Aside from acting, Fedelin has pursued music. He released his first single, "Kundi Ikaw," and later launched his five-track debut extended play, Caution, in 2024. The EP included original compositions such as "Mamahalin Kita" and "Iba Ka."

His career demonstrates a transition from reality-show contestant to television and film actor, while his music releases show his interest in developing a parallel career as a recording artist.
