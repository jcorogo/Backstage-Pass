About Richard Gutierrez

Richard Gutierrez, born Richard Kristian Rama Gutierrez on January 21, 1984, in Los Angeles, California, is a Filipino actor, model, and producer. He became one of the most recognizable leading men in Philippine television through fantasy series, action dramas, and romantic productions.

Gutierrez comes from the well-known Gutierrez entertainment family. His parents are actors Eddie Gutierrez and Annabelle Rama. He began appearing in entertainment projects as a child, gaining early exposure to film and television.

His career developed through youth-oriented programs and supporting roles. He became more widely recognized after joining GMA Network’s roster of young actors and appearing in television productions such as Click and Ikaw Lang ang Mamahalin.

His major breakthrough came through Mulawin (2004), where he portrayed Aguiluz opposite Angel Locsin. The fantasy series became a major television success and established him as one of GMA’s leading actors. He reprised the role in Mulawin: The Movie.

Gutierrez went on to star in a series of fantasy and action productions, including Sugo, Captain Barbell, Lupin, Kamandag, Codename: Asero, and Zorro. His work in these programs earned him the popular reputation of a fantasy-series leading man.

His film credits include The Promise, When I Met You, Patient X, My Lady Boss, Unbreakable, and Fantastica. His roles have ranged from romance and comedy to horror and action.

After moving to ABS-CBN projects, he appeared in La Luna Sangre, FPJ’s Ang Probinsyano, and The Iron Heart. These productions gave him opportunities to portray more mature and complicated characters.

Gutierrez has also pursued physical training, including karate and mixed martial arts. He has been associated with environmental advocacy and has participated in projects supporting conservation.

His career represents a major era of Philippine fantasy television. From Mulawin and Captain Barbell to later action dramas, he has remained a familiar figure in the country’s entertainment industry.
