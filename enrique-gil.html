About Enrique Gil

Enrique Gil, whose full name is Enrique Mari Bacay Gil V, is a Filipino actor, dancer, and model born on March 30, 1992, in Cebu City. He became widely recognized for his romantic leading roles and energetic screen presence.

Gil began his entertainment career in 2008, appearing in commercials and television projects. He gradually gained acting opportunities through youth-oriented programs and supporting roles. His early television work included Pieta, Mula sa Puso, and Budoy.

His popularity grew through Princess and I, where he played a supporting character, and Muling Buksan ang Puso. However, his defining breakthrough came in 2014 with Forevermore, in which he portrayed Alexander "Xander" Grande III opposite Liza Soberano. The series became a major success and established their partnership as one of the country’s most popular romantic pairings.

The success of Forevermore led to films such as The Bet, Just the Way You Are, Everyday I Love You, and My Ex and Whys. His chemistry with Soberano helped make their films commercially successful and expanded their popularity beyond television.

Gil also appeared in other projects that allowed him to explore different characters, including She’s the One, Dukot, and Alone/Together. His performances often combine romance, comedy, and emotional drama.

Beyond acting, Gil is known for dancing and performing. His energetic style became part of his appeal, especially in promotional appearances, variety programs, and entertainment events.

After a period of more selective project appearances, he continued pursuing acting and other professional opportunities. His career remains closely associated with the success of Forevermore and the films he made with Liza Soberano.

Gil’s contribution to Philippine entertainment is particularly significant in the development of the modern romantic leading man: charismatic, physically expressive, and capable of carrying both lighthearted and emotional stories.

Enrique Mari Bacay Gil V was born on March 30, 1992, in Cebu City, Philippines. He began working in entertainment at a young age, appearing in commercials and music videos before developing a career as a television and film actor. His early television work included appearances in youth-oriented programs and supporting roles that allowed him to gain experience in front of the camera.

His career developed through ABS-CBN projects such as Mula sa Puso, Budoy, and Princess and I. His major breakthrough as a leading man came when he was paired with Liza Soberano in Forevermore. The success of the series created the LizQuen love team, which became one of the most recognizable romantic pairings of its generation. Their subsequent projects included Dolce Amore, Bagani, and the films Just the Way You Are, Everyday I Love You, My Ex and Whys, and Alone/Together.

Gil also demonstrated his range through comedy, drama, and action-oriented roles. His television series Make It with You, which reunited him with Soberano, was cut short in 2020 during the pandemic. He later explained that the extended interruption made him consider leaving show business and enjoying a more ordinary life. In 2026, he returned to television through the action-comedy series A Secret in Prague, marking a new phase in his career.
