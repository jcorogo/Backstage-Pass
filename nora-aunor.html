About Nora Aunor

Nora Cabaltera Villamayor, professionally known as Nora Aunor, was born on May 21, 1953, in Iriga, Camarines Sur. She came from a financially struggling family and experienced a difficult childhood. She helped support her family by selling peanuts and bottled water at bus terminals and railway stations. Despite these circumstances, she developed a passion for singing and entered amateur singing competitions.

Her major breakthrough came in 1967 when she won the national finals of Tawag ng Tanghalan. Her victory introduced her to a nationwide audience and opened the door to a recording career. Her songs became enormously popular, and she developed a devoted following. Her on-screen partnership with Tirso Cruz III, popularly known as Guy and Pip, also became a major entertainment phenomenon.

Aunor eventually moved from musical performances and commercial films into more demanding dramatic roles. She starred in acclaimed films including Bona, Himala, Tatlong Taong Walang Diyos, The Flor Contemplacion Story, Naglalayag, Thy Womb, and Taklub. Her portrayal of Elsa in Himala became one of the most iconic performances in Philippine cinema, while Thy Womb brought her major international recognition.

Her achievements include numerous FAMAS and Gawad Urian awards, an Asia Pacific Screen Award, and an Asian Film Award for her performance in Thy Womb. She was named a National Artist of the Philippines for Film and Broadcast Arts in 2022. Nora Aunor passed away on April 16, 2025, at the age of 71. Her legacy continues through her films, recordings, and influence on generations of Filipino performers.

Source: asiapacificscreenawards.com
