About Shaina Magdayao

Shaina Garcia Magdayao, professionally known as Shaina Magdayao, is a Filipino actress, dancer, and television personality. Born on November 6, 1989, in Quezon City, she began her career as a child actress and developed into a respected performer in film and television drama.

Magdayao entered show business at a young age, appearing in commercials and children’s television. One of her earliest major opportunities was the television series Lyra, which helped introduce her to audiences as a young leading actress.

She later became known through Ang TV, G-Mik, and other youth-oriented programs. Her transition into mature roles was gradual, allowing her to develop a broader range of acting abilities.

Her television credits include Marina, Kampanerang Kuba, Lobo, Kahit Isang Saglit, The Two of Us, Nathaniel, and The Better Half. She also appeared in The Good Son, where she portrayed a character involved in a complicated family story.

Magdayao’s film work includes Tanging Yaman, Feng Shui, Ang Tanging Ina, Four Sisters and a Wedding, Ang Tanging Ina Mo: Last na ’To!, and The Third Party. Her performance in Ang Tanging Ina and its sequels showed her ability to combine comedy with family-oriented storytelling.

She has also received recognition for more serious film work, including Ang Huling Henya and independent productions that allowed her to explore different character types. Her acting achievements include awards and nominations from organizations such as FAMAS, Gawad Pasado, and the Young Critics Circle.

Outside acting, Magdayao has pursued dancing and other performance activities. Her long career has allowed her to move from child television into mature drama without losing her identity as a versatile performer.

Her contribution to Philippine entertainment is marked by her longevity, steady acting development, and ability to participate in both mainstream and more demanding dramatic projects.
