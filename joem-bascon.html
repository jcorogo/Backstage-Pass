About Joem Bascon

Joem Bascon, whose full name is Joseph Emmanuel Bascon, is a Filipino actor known for his work in television dramas, independent films, and suspense productions. Born on August 29, 1986, in Mandaluyong, he developed a reputation as a versatile performer capable of handling both supporting and leading roles.

Bascon entered the entertainment industry in the mid-2000s and began appearing in film and television productions. His early film work included All About Love, Batanes: Sa Dulo ng Walang Hanggan, and Adela. These projects helped him develop experience in different genres.

His horror film work includes Shake, Rattle & Roll 11 (2009), White House (2010), and Aswang (2011). In Shake, Rattle & Roll 11, he appeared in a story within the long-running Filipino horror anthology. White House involved a group of people who encounter supernatural events inside an allegedly haunted mansion.

Bascon also appeared in Haunted Mansion (2015), a horror film about young people who encounter disturbing supernatural occurrences during a school retreat. These roles helped establish his familiarity with Filipino horror and suspense storytelling.

Outside horror, he has appeared in Heneral Luna, 10,000 Hours, Blue Bustamante, The Bride and the Lover, The Broken Marriage Vow, Dirty Linen, Flower of Evil, and The Iron Heart. His television performances often involve complex supporting characters, antagonists, and emotionally troubled individuals.

He has continued working across television, film, and streaming projects, including Incognito and Mga Batang Riles. His career demonstrates his ability to move between independent cinema and mainstream entertainment.

Bascon’s place in this category comes from his recurring involvement in supernatural and suspense productions, combined with his broader reputation as a dependable character actor.
