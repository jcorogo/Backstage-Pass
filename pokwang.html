Pokwang

About Pokwang

Marietta Tan Subong, professionally known as Pokwang, is a Filipino actress, comedian, television host, and singer. She became one of the country’s recognizable female comedy performers through her energetic personality, expressive humor, character impersonations, and ability to connect with audiences.

Before entering mainstream entertainment, Subong experienced a difficult early life and worked abroad, including employment as a performer in Japan. She later returned to the Philippines and pursued opportunities in comedy bars, where she developed her stage persona and comedic style.

Her breakthrough came in 2004 when she joined and won ABS-CBN’s Clown in a Million, a comedy talent-search segment of Yes, Yes Show!. The victory helped her secure opportunities in television and introduced her to a nationwide audience.

She became a regular performer in ABS-CBN programs, appearing in Maalaala Mo Kaya, Krystala, Komiks Presents: Da Adventures of Pedro Penduko, Banana Split, and 100 Days to Heaven. She also became a television host through programs such as Wowowee, Pilipinas Win na Win, and Happy Yipee Yehey!.

Her sitcom work includes Aalog-Alog and Toda Max, with the latter becoming one of her best-known comedy projects. She also appeared in films such as Agent X44, SupahPapalicious, A Mother’s Story, My Illegal Wife, and Wang Fam.

Pokwang has demonstrated that her abilities extend beyond comedy. She received critical recognition for her dramatic performance in Oda sa Wala (2018), where she portrayed a woman dealing with grief and loneliness. The role earned her major acting recognition and demonstrated her capacity for emotionally restrained performances.

She later starred in Becky and Badette (2023) alongside Eugene Domingo. Her work in comedy and drama has continued to establish her as a versatile actress.

Her career is particularly significant because she successfully transformed her experiences in comedy bars into a long-running career in mainstream entertainment.
