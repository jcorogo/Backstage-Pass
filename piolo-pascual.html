About Piolo Pascual

Piolo Jose Nonato Pascual, professionally known as Piolo Pascual, is a Filipino actor, singer, model, and film producer. Born on January 12, 1977, in Manila, he developed a career that combines romantic leading roles with dramatic performances in television, independent films, and major motion pictures.

Pascual initially pursued entertainment opportunities while still young. He gained early exposure through television and eventually became part of the ABS-CBN talent roster. His early work included appearances in That’s Entertainment and supporting roles in film and television before he established himself as a leading actor.

His career developed through productions such as Esperanza, Sa Puso Ko, Iingatan Ka, and Pangako Sa ’Yo. He became especially popular through his onscreen partnership with Judy Ann Santos, with whom he appeared in films such as Don’t Give Up on Us and Till There Was You.

Pascual’s filmography includes Milan, Dekada ’70, Dreamboy, Don’t Give Up on Us, Paano Kita Iibigin, The Breakup Playlist, and Starting Over Again. He has also taken on challenging dramatic and historical roles in films such as On the Job, Hele sa Hiwagang Hapis, GomBurZa, and Mallari.

His more recent work includes The Kingdom (2024), The Ride (2025), and Manila’s Finest (2025). These projects demonstrate his continued involvement in major Philippine film productions and his willingness to explore different genres.

As a recording artist, Pascual has released albums and performed concerts, combining his acting career with music. He has also developed a role as a producer, participating in projects through his production work.

Pascual’s career is distinguished by his longevity, versatility, and ability to move between romantic drama, historical films, action, and independent cinema. He remains one of the most recognizable leading men in Philippine entertainment.
