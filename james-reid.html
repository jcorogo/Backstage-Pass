About James Reid

Robert James Reid born on May 11, 1993, in Sydney, Australia, is a Filipino-Australian actor, singer, songwriter, dancer, and record producer. He became famous after winning Pinoy Big Brother: Teen Clash 2010 and later developed a career in acting and contemporary Filipino pop music.

Reid moved to the Philippines during his teenage years. His participation in Pinoy Big Brother introduced him to a national audience, and winning the competition provided the foundation for his entertainment career.

His early projects included television appearances and music collaborations. He began recording with Bret Jackson as part of the duo We Are Whatever and later released solo music.

His interest in pop, R&B, and electronic sounds eventually became an important part of his identity as an artist.

His acting breakthrough arrived in 2014 with Diary ng Panget, where he starred opposite Nadine Lustre. The film became a major commercial success and launched the JaDine love team. They followed it with Talk Back and You’re Dead, Para sa Hopeless Romantic, and This Time.

The pair’s television popularity expanded through On the Wings of Love (2015–2016), a romantic drama that became one of the defining programs of their careers. They later reunited in Till I Met You.

Reid continued to develop his music independently. His releases include Palm Dreams and Lovescene, while his musical direction increasingly incorporated modern pop and R&B influences. He also became involved in music production and artist development through Careless Music, a company associated with his creative work.

His later acting projects include Miss Granny, The Romance of Tiger and Rose was not among his Philippine acting credits, and other selected productions. He gradually shifted more attention toward music and creative production.

Reid’s career is significant because he helped connect mainstream Filipino entertainment with contemporary pop and R&B music. His work with Nadine Lustre also created one of the most recognizable romantic partnerships of the 2010s.

James Reid was born on May 11, 1993, in Sydney, Australia. He moved to the Philippines with his father when he was 15 years old. His entry into Philippine entertainment came through the reality program Pinoy Big Brother: Teen Clash 2010, where he emerged as the season’s Big Winner. The exposure gave him opportunities in television, film, music, and commercial endorsements.

Reid began developing his acting career through projects such as Good Vibes and My Music Story. His major breakthrough came in 2014 when he starred opposite Nadine Lustre in Diary ng Panget. The film’s popularity established the JaDine love team, and the pair continued their successful partnership in Talk Back and You’re Dead, Para sa Hopeless Romantic, This Time, and the television series On the Wings of Love.

In addition to acting, Reid became a recording artist with an interest in pop, R&B, and contemporary music. His releases include his self-titled album and Palm Dreams. He later founded Careless Music, a music and talent company that supported his work as an artist and creative entrepreneur. His partnership with Lustre became one of the most prominent romantic pairings of the 2010s, while his later career increasingly focused on music production and independent creative projects.
