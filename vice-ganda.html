About Vice Ganda

Jose Marie Borja Viceral, professionally known as Vice Ganda, is a Filipino comedian, actor, television host, singer, and entrepreneur. Born on March 31, 1976, in Tondo, Manila, he became one of the most influential comedy performers in contemporary Philippine entertainment.

Vice Ganda began performing in comedy bars in Manila, particularly at The Punchline and The Library. Before achieving mainstream recognition, he developed his stage persona through stand-up comedy, observational humor, impersonations, satire, and jokes about everyday Filipino experiences. These early performances helped him build a loyal audience and sharpen his ability to connect with people through spontaneous humor.

His breakthrough came when he joined ABS-CBN’s It’s Showtime in 2009. His comedic exchanges with contestants, hosts, and studio audiences quickly became a major attraction of the program. His popularity eventually led to opportunities in television acting and feature films.

He made his first major film appearance as the lead character in Petrang Kabayo (2010). He followed this with a succession of commercially successful comedy films, including The Unkabogable Praybeyt Benjamin, Sisterakas, Girl, Boy, Bakla, Tomboy, The Amazing Praybeyt Benjamin, Beauty and the Bestie, The Super Parental Guardians, and Gandarrapiddo: The Revenger Squad. Several of these became major box-office hits, establishing him as one of the country’s biggest comedy-film attractions.

Vice Ganda also pursued music, releasing recordings and performing in concerts. His comedy frequently incorporates social commentary, popular culture, gender expression, relationships, and observations about Filipino society.

His career is notable for bringing comedy-bar performance into mainstream television and blockbuster cinema. He has also become an influential public personality whose humor and visibility have contributed to discussions about representation and acceptance.
