About Ken Chan

Ken Chan, born Ken Steven Angeles Chan on January 17, 1993, is a Filipino actor, model, and television personality known for his work in GMA Network dramas and romantic television productions.

Chan entered the entertainment industry through auditions and acting opportunities during his younger years. He began appearing in television and film projects in the early 2010s, initially taking supporting roles while developing his acting skills.

He gained early recognition through youth-oriented productions such as Tween Hearts and television dramas including Biritera, Yesterday’s Bride, and Rhodora X. These appearances helped him establish a steady presence in GMA’s programming.

His career took a major step forward with Destiny Rose (2015–2016), where he portrayed Joey Vergara, a transgender woman who undergoes a personal journey of identity and acceptance. The role was a major challenge and attracted considerable public attention. It also demonstrated Chan’s willingness to take on a character that required a substantial transformation in performance and presentation.

He continued with Meant to Be (2017), a romantic comedy series in which he starred alongside Barbie Forteza, Jak Roberto, and Ivan Dorschner. The program became popular and strengthened his position as a leading actor.

Another important project was My Special Tatay (2018–2019), where he portrayed Boyet, a young father with an intellectual disability who faces challenges while raising his child. The series allowed Chan to explore a sensitive dramatic role and earned him further recognition.

His other television credits include The Cure, Ang Dalawang Ikaw, Mano Po Legacy: Her Big Boss, and Abot-Kamay na Pangarap. His film appearances include Tween Academy: Class of 2012, Just One Summer, This Time I’ll Be Sweeter, Huling Ulan sa Tag-Araw, and Papa Mascot.

Chan has also worked as a television host and performer, appearing in variety and entertainment programs. His career is particularly remembered for his transformative performances in Destiny Rose and My Special Tatay.

His contribution to Philippine entertainment lies in his versatility and his willingness to portray characters that move beyond conventional romantic leading-man roles.
