About Cherry Pie Picache

Cherry Pie Sison Picache, professionally known as Cherry Pie Picache, is a Filipino actress recognized for her work in independent films, mainstream cinema, and television dramas. Born on May 27, 1970, in Metro Manila, she developed a reputation as a versatile character actress capable of delivering emotionally powerful performances.

Picache studied at St. Mary’s College and St. Paul University before pursuing dentistry at Centro Escolar University. Although she completed her dentistry studies, she developed a professional career in entertainment through acting and commercial work.

She began appearing in television and film productions during the late 1980s and 1990s. Her television work included Familia Zaragoza, while her early film appearances helped establish her as a reliable supporting performer.

Her acting breakthrough became more visible through films such as American Adobo, Bridal Shower, Feng Shui, Manay Po!, Kaleldo, and Foster Child. These projects demonstrated her ability to portray women from different backgrounds and social circumstances.

Her performance in Bridal Shower received major critical recognition, while Foster Child and Kaleldo further strengthened her standing in Philippine independent cinema. She received acting awards from organizations and film festivals, including the Gawad Urian, Durban International Film Festival, and Osian’s Cinefan Festival.

Picache has also appeared in numerous television dramas, including Saan Ka Man Naroroon, Tayong Dalawa, Noah, My Binondo Girl, Ina, Kapatid, Anak, Ikaw Lamang, On the Wings of Love, The Blood Sisters, and FPJ’s Batang Quiapo.

She is particularly recognized for portraying mothers, family matriarchs, and emotionally complicated supporting characters. Her ability to make these characters believable has made her a valuable presence in both film and television.

Picache’s career demonstrates the importance of experienced character actors in Philippine entertainment. Her body of work includes independent films, award-winning dramas, and major commercial productions.
