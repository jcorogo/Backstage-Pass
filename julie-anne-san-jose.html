About Julie Anne San Jose

Julie Anne San Jose is a Filipino singer, songwriter, actress, and television personality. Born Julie Anne Peñaflorida San Jose on May 17, 1994, in Novaliches, Quezon City, she became one of the country’s prominent recording artists while also developing a successful acting career.

San Jose showed an interest in music at a very young age. She began singing as a child and appeared on television through Batibot and the Little Miss Philippines segment of Eat Bulaga!.

Her wider exposure came in 2005 when she joined the singing competition Popstar Kids. Although she did not win the competition, she became part of the children’s singing group Sugarpop. The group performed on GMA’s Sunday variety program SOP, giving her an opportunity to gain experience as a recording and television performer.

After Sugarpop, San Jose continued as a solo artist and became part of Party Pilipinas. Her music career expanded with the release of her self-titled album in 2012, which became a major commercial success. The album produced popular songs and helped establish her as a prominent young recording artist.

She became known for her vocal range, musical versatility, and ability to perform pop, R&B, and acoustic music. Her recording achievements include multiple Awit Awards, Myx Music Awards, and PMPC Star Awards for Music. She has also received major recognition from the Philippine Association of the Record Industry.

San Jose eventually expanded into acting. Her television credits include Kahit Nasaan Ka Man, Buena Familia, Pinulot Ka Lang sa Lupa, My Guitar Princess, and Heartful Café. Her most widely recognized dramatic role came in Maria Clara at Ibarra (2022–2023), where she portrayed Maria Clara, one of the central characters in the historical series inspired by José Rizal’s novels.
