About Kyle Echarri

Kyle Echarri is a Filipino-American actor, singer, and songwriter. Born Kyle John Paradillo Echarri on June 20, 2003, in Orange County, California, he grew up in the United States before moving with his family to Cebu, Philippines.

Echarri developed an interest in music at an early age. His singing abilities became widely known when he joined the second season of The Voice Kids Philippines in 2015. Although he did not win the competition, his performances attracted attention and introduced him to a national audience.

After the program, he pursued a recording career and released his debut album, It’s Me Kyle, in 2016. He followed it with additional music releases, including Kyle Echarri and New Views. His music has explored pop and contemporary styles, allowing him to establish an identity as a young recording artist.

He eventually expanded into acting, appearing in television productions such as On the Wings of Love, Kadenang Ginto, and Lyric and Beat. His role in Kadenang Ginto helped him become more familiar to television audiences and demonstrated his ability to perform in dramatic roles.

Echarri later appeared in Beach Bros, The Iron Heart, Senior High, and Pamilya Sagrado. These projects gave him opportunities to portray more mature and complex characters, including roles involving action, suspense, and family conflict.

He has also participated in theatrical productions, including Ako Ay si Josephine and Ballet Philippines’ Awitin Mo at Isasayaw Ko. His theater work demonstrates an interest in live performance beyond television and recording.

Echarri has continued developing his music career alongside acting. His collaboration with Illest Morena on the song "Believe" for Coke Studio further reflected his interest in working with other contemporary musicians.

His career is notable for its combination of singing, acting, and stage performance, making him one of the younger entertainers who have developed in multiple areas of Philippine show business.
