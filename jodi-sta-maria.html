About Jodi Sta. Maria

Jodi Chrissie Garcia Sta. Maria, professionally known as Jodi Sta. Maria, is a Filipino actress, producer, and television personality. Born on June 16, 1982, she has built a career spanning youth-oriented television, romantic dramas, psychological thrillers, and emotionally demanding family stories.

Sta. Maria began acting in the late 1990s and became part of ABS-CBN’s Star Magic talent roster. Her early appearances included youth-oriented programs such as Ang TV and G-Mik. These projects helped her gain experience before she transitioned into more mature television roles.

She became widely recognized through Tabing Ilog, where she portrayed Georgia. The program became a popular youth drama and helped establish her as one of the familiar young actresses of her generation.

Her television career expanded through productions such as Pangako Sa ’Yo, Hiram, Maging Sino Ka Man, Sana Maulit Muli, Be Careful With My Heart, and The Story of Us. Her performance in Be Careful With My Heart introduced her to a broader audience through a lighter romantic story, while later projects gave her more demanding dramatic roles.

One of her most important performances came in The Broken Marriage Vow (2022), the Philippine adaptation of the British series Doctor Foster. She portrayed Dr. Jill Ilustre, a woman whose marriage begins to collapse after discovering her husband’s infidelity. The role required a wide range of emotions, from grief and anger to determination and personal recovery.

She also starred in Sisa, The Achy Breaky Hearts, Mano Po 7: Chinoy, Labyu with an Accent, and Lavender Fields. These projects demonstrate her ability to move between film, television drama, romance, and suspense.

Sta. Maria has received several acting awards and nominations. She is also known for her dedication to personal development and education, having pursued further studies while maintaining her acting career.

Her career is distinguished by her gradual transformation from a youth television performer into one of Philippine television’s prominent dramatic actresses.
