Joonee Gamboa

About Joonee Gamboa

Joonee Gamboa is a veteran Filipino actor whose career includes film, television, theater, and voice acting. He first developed his craft on the stage, performing in productions associated with the Philippine Educational Theater Association (PETA). He became part of the generation of Filipino theater performers who helped establish a stronger foundation for modern Philippine stage productions.

Gamboa entered the film industry after encouragement from director Lino Brocka. His first movie was Santiago! in 1970, starring Fernando Poe Jr. He later became known for his powerful supporting performances, particularly in films that required authoritative, serious, or emotionally complex characters.

His notable film appearances include Burlesk Queen, Pagputi ng Uwak, Pag-itim ng Tagak, Enter the Ninja, Bayaning 3rd World, Feng Shui, and Ang Panday. He won the Best Supporting Actor award at the 1977 Metro Manila Film Festival for Burlesk Queen and received further recognition for Pagputi ng Uwak, Pag-itim ng Tagak.

Gamboa also became known for voice acting. He provided English-language voices for characters in the animated series Voltes V and served as narrator for the documentary Asia’s Titanic. In later years, he continued accepting television and film roles, including work in FPJ’s Batang Quiapo. His long career reflects his continuing dedication to acting and the performing arts.
