About Vic Sotto

Marvic Valentin Castelo Sotto, widely known as Vic Sotto, is a Filipino actor, comedian, television host, producer, and director. Born on April 28, 1954, he became one of the country’s most recognizable entertainers through decades of comedy, television hosting, and film work.

Sotto began his career in music as a member of the musical group VST & Company, which became popular during the disco era of the 1970s. The group produced well-known Original Pilipino Music recordings and helped establish his presence in the entertainment industry before he became a major television comedian.

He later became part of the comedy group that included his brother Tito Sotto and Joey de Leon. Together, they became associated with the long-running television program Eat Bulaga!, where Sotto developed his reputation for humor, improvisation, and comedic timing.

His film career includes numerous comedy productions, many of which became commercial successes. Among his well-known projects are the Enteng Kabisote films, Okay Ka, Fairy Ko!, Pak! Pak! My Dr. Kwak!, and Si Agimat at si Enteng Kabisote. These movies frequently combined comedy, fantasy, adventure, and action, making him familiar to audiences beyond traditional sitcom and variety-show formats.

Sotto has also been involved in production and entertainment management. His work behind the scenes helped sustain his long-running film and television projects.

Outside show business, he pursued a political career and served as a senator. His official Senate biography also recognizes his work as a composer, musician, actor, and television personality.

Sotto’s legacy is especially strong in Philippine comedy and television. His decades of work have made him one of the most enduring figures in local entertainment.
