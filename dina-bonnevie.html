About Dina Bonnevie

Geraldine Schaer Bonnevie, popularly known as Dina Bonnevie, was born on January 27, 1961, in Quezon City. She grew up with an interest in performing arts and participated in drama activities during her school years. She attended St. Theresa’s College in Manila and later completed her elementary and secondary education in Legazpi City. She also pursued Communication Arts studies at Ateneo de Manila University.

Bonnevie entered the entertainment industry in the late 1970s after participating in the Miss Magnolia beauty contest. Her early film appearances included Under-age and Temptation Island in 1980. She became a major star through Katorse, which helped establish her as a prominent actress in Philippine cinema.

During the 1980s and 1990s, she became known for intense dramatic performances. Her notable films include Tinik sa Dibdib, Magdusa Ka!, Ang Babaeng Nawawala sa Sarili, Gumapang Ka sa Lusak, and Tatarin. Her performance in Magdusa Ka! earned her major acting awards, including FAMAS and Film Academy of the Philippines recognition.

Bonnevie also developed a successful television career, appearing in dramas, family series, and comedy programs. She became particularly associated with mature and emotionally challenging roles, earning the nickname Drama Queen. Her decades of experience and ability to move between film and television have made her one of the most recognizable veteran actresses in the Philippines.
