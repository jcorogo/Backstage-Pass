About Sofia Pablo

Sofia Pablo is a Filipino actress, singer, dancer, and social-media personality. Born Sofia Beatrice Hijazi Pablo on April 10, 2006, in Manila, she became known as one of the young performers of GMA Network’s newer generation.

Pablo began appearing in television at a young age. Her early acting work included an appearance in the series Because of You, followed by roles in programs such as Tadhana, Magpakailanman, and Wish Ko Lang. These early opportunities allowed her to develop experience in different kinds of television storytelling.

She gained wider recognition through Oh, My Mama! (2016), where she appeared as Nicole. Her performance helped establish her as a young actress with the potential to take on more substantial roles.

One of her most important projects was Prima Donnas, in which she portrayed Len-Len. The series centered on three sisters and their complicated family circumstances. Pablo’s performance helped make her familiar to audiences who regularly followed GMA’s afternoon drama programs.

She later appeared in Luv Is: Caught in His Arms, where she portrayed Florence and worked with fellow young actor Allen Ansay. Their on-screen pairing attracted attention among younger viewers and helped expand her popularity.

Her other television appearances include Destined to Be Yours, Dear Uge, Regal Studio Presents, and Open 24/7. She has also explored singing, dancing, and online content creation, developing a following on social-media platforms.

Pablo’s career is still developing, but her progress from child performer to leading young actress demonstrates her growing versatility. Her ability to combine acting with music, dancing, and digital entertainment makes her one of the recognizable young talents in GMA Network’s roster.
