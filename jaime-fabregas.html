About Jaime Fabregas

Jaime Francisco García Fábregas, professionally known as Jaime Fabregas, was born on February 28, 1950, in Iriga City, Camarines Sur. He is a Filipino actor and composer whose career combines dramatic performance with musical composition. Before becoming a familiar face in Philippine television and film, he developed his acting abilities through theater, including work with Repertory Philippines.

Fabregas became known for his versatility as a character actor. He appeared in major films such as Oro, Plata, Mata, Misteryo sa Tuwa, and Here Comes the Bride. His ability to portray authority figures, family patriarchs, military officers, and complex supporting characters made him a regular presence in Philippine productions.

He also established an important career as a film composer and musical scorer. His work on Misteryo sa Tuwa marked an important early step in this aspect of his career. Over time, he received numerous nominations and awards for his musical contributions, including recognition from FAMAS, Gawad Urian, and the Metro Manila Film Festival.

A later generation of television audiences became familiar with him through his portrayal of General Delfin Borja in FPJ’s Ang Probinsyano. Beyond acting and composing, Fabregas has supported the preservation of Filipino cinema through his involvement with Sagip Pelikula, emphasizing the importance of restoring and archiving classic films for future generations.
