About Michael V.

Beethoven del Valle Bunagan, professionally known as Michael V. or Bitoy, is a Filipino comedian, actor, writer, director, singer, rapper, and television host. Born on December 17, 1969, in Pandacan, Manila, he is recognized for his versatility, character impersonations, parody songs, and contributions to Philippine sketch comedy.

Michael V. initially explored music and became known as a rapper and parody performer during the early 1990s. His humorous musical approach helped him develop a distinctive identity before he entered mainstream television. He also completed a degree in Mass Communication at the Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila.

His television career began with appearances in variety and entertainment programs. He eventually became a cast member of Bubble Gang in 1995, one of the longest-running comedy programs in Philippine television. The show provided him with a platform to develop recurring characters, impersonations, satirical sketches, and musical parodies.

Michael V. became equally recognizable through Pepito Manaloto, which premiered in 2010. He plays the title character, Pepito, an ordinary man whose life changes after winning a lottery. The sitcom became a major part of his career and allowed him to demonstrate a more family-oriented style of comedy.

His other projects include Yaya & Angelina: The Spoiled Brat Movie, Bitoy’s Funniest Videos, and the film Family History (2019), which he directed and starred in. His work behind the camera demonstrates his involvement in storytelling, writing, and production.

Michael V. has received numerous awards for his comedy performances, including repeated recognition from the Asian Television Awards. His influence comes from his ability to combine writing, acting, parody, and direction, making him one of the most versatile comedic entertainers in the country.
