About Belle Mariano

Belle Mariano, whose full name is Belinda Angelito Mariano, is a Filipino actress and singer. Born on June 10, 2002, in Pasig, she began working in entertainment as a child and gradually developed into one of ABS-CBN’s prominent young leading actresses.

Mariano started appearing in television commercials and supporting roles at approximately nine years old. She joined the children’s comedy program Goin’ Bulilit, where she worked alongside other young performers who later became familiar faces in Philippine television.

Her early acting credits included Lorenzo’s Time, Maalaala Mo Kaya, and supporting appearances in films such as Rekorder, Can’t Help Falling in Love, and Love You to the Stars and Back. These projects gave her opportunities to portray different characters before receiving her first major leading role.

Her breakthrough as a leading actress came through He’s Into Her (2021), where she portrayed Maxpein del Valle opposite Donny Pangilinan. The series became popular among younger audiences and established the DonBelle pairing as one of the most recognizable contemporary love teams.

The success of the series led to a second season and further projects together. Mariano and Pangilinan later starred in Love Is Color Blind and Can’t Buy Me Love, the latter becoming a major television project for the pair.

Her other film credits include Four Sisters Before the Wedding, An Inconvenient Love, and Under Parallel Skies. She has also developed a music career through Star Pop, releasing songs and performing as a recording artist.

In 2025, Mariano renewed her contract with ABS-CBN and announced plans involving a new album and the film Meet, Greet & Bye. Her career has developed from child acting to leading television and film roles, while her work in music has expanded her identity beyond acting.
