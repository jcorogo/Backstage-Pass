About Lorna Tolentino

Victoria Lorna Perez Aluquin, professionally known as Lorna Tolentino, was born on December 23, 1961. She began her entertainment career as a child actress during the early 1970s. Her first film appearances included My Little One and Divina Gracia. She eventually adopted the screen name Lorna Tolentino, which became associated with one of the most enduring acting careers in Philippine cinema.

During the 1970s and 1980s, Tolentino gradually transitioned from youthful roles into mature dramatic performances. She became one of the most respected actresses of her generation through films such as Brutal, Salome, Moral, Bayan Ko: Kapit sa Patalim, and Narito ang Puso Ko. These productions demonstrated her ability to portray complex women and characters dealing with difficult social and personal circumstances.

Her career continued through major television and film projects, including Abakada... Ina, Only Yours, Lobo, Dahil May Isang Ikaw, Juan dela Cruz, and The General’s Daughter. She became known for her strong dramatic performances, often portraying mothers, influential family figures, and women with complicated personal histories.

Tolentino has received numerous acting awards, including recognition from FAMAS, the Film Academy of the Philippines, and other major Philippine award-giving bodies. She was married to actor Rudy Fernandez until his death in 2008. Her long career, extensive filmography, and consistent television presence have established her as one of the country’s most respected veteran actresses.
