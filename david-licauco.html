About David Licauco

David Licauco, whose full name is David Alexander Sy Licauco, is a Filipino actor, model, and entrepreneur. Born on June 15, 1994, in Quezon City, he became one of GMA Network’s prominent young leading men through historical drama, romance, and television series.

Before becoming widely recognized as an actor, Licauco worked as a model and pursued business interests. He studied at De La Salle–College of Saint Benilde and became involved in entrepreneurship while developing his entertainment career.

He began taking acting opportunities in the mid-2010s, appearing in supporting and guest roles. One of his early major television appearances was in Mulawin vs. Ravena (2017), where he portrayed Malik. He later appeared in productions such as The One That Got Away, Beautiful Justice, and Heartful Café.

His major breakthrough came through Maria Clara at Ibarra (2022–2023), where he portrayed Fidel de los Reyes y Maglipol. The historical fantasy series became a major success, and his character’s development and romantic storyline attracted considerable audience attention. The role significantly expanded his popularity and established him as a leading actor.

Licauco continued with Maging Sino Ka Man (2023), where he played Carding, and Pulang Araw (2024), a historical drama set during the Japanese occupation of the Philippines. These projects allowed him to portray characters in different historical and dramatic settings.

Beyond acting, Licauco has pursued several business ventures, including food and lifestyle-related enterprises. He has also spoken publicly about the importance of discipline, personal development, and balancing his entertainment career with entrepreneurship.

His career is notable for his progression from modeling and supporting roles to major television characters. His portrayal of Fidel in Maria Clara at Ibarra remains one of the defining performances that established his popularity among Filipino audiences.
