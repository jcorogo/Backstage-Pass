About Heart Evangelista

Love Marie Payawal Ongpauco-Escudero, professionally known as Heart Evangelista, is a Filipino actress, television host, visual artist, fashion personality, and entrepreneur. Born on February 14, 1985, in Manila, she grew up in a family associated with the restaurant business before pursuing a career in entertainment.

Evangelista was discovered by a talent scout while still young and became part of ABS-CBN’s Star Circle talent program. She adopted the screen name Heart Evangelista and began appearing in youth-oriented television programs. One of her early projects was G-mik, which introduced her to young audiences.

She gradually developed her acting career through television dramas and films. Her credits include Hiram, Berks, Panday, Luna Mystika, Full House, Legacy, and Juan dela Cruz. She also appeared in films such as Trip, Mano Po 6: A Mother’s Love, Ay Ayeng, and The Third Party.

Evangelista’s work in drama allowed her to portray a range of characters, from romantic leads to women facing complicated family and personal conflicts. She received recognition for her performances in film and television, including awards from local entertainment organizations.

Beyond acting, she has established herself as a visual artist. Her paintings, particularly her works on luxury bags and other surfaces, have attracted international attention. She has also become a prominent fashion figure, attending major fashion events and collaborating with international luxury brands.

Her public identity expanded further through her marriage to politician Francis Escudero and her involvement in charitable and animal-welfare initiatives. She has used her platform to support various advocacy projects.

Evangelista’s career is an example of a performer who successfully expanded from teen television into acting, visual art, fashion, and entrepreneurship.
