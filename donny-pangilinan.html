About Donny Pangilinan

Donny Pangilinan, whose full name is Donato Antonio Laxa Pangilinan, is a Filipino actor, model, singer, host, and former video jockey. Born on February 10, 1998, he comes from a family with a background in entertainment and public life.

He is the son of actor Maricel Laxa and public speaker Anthony Pangilinan. Despite his family background, he developed his own career through television hosting, modeling, and acting opportunities.

Pangilinan began working in entertainment as a MYX video jockey in 2016. He also appeared in ABS-CBN programs and became a performer and host on ASAP. These early experiences helped him gain familiarity with television production and develop his public personality.

His film debut came through Walwal (2018), followed by appearances in To Love Some Buddy and Fantastica. His first major leading role came in James & Pat & Dave (2020), where he played Dave Verona.

His popularity increased considerably through He’s Into Her (2021), in which he portrayed Deib Lohr Enrile opposite Belle Mariano. The series became a major streaming success and established their pairing, known as DonBelle. Their partnership continued in Love Is Color Blind and the film An Inconvenient Love.

Pangilinan also starred in Can’t Buy Me Love (2023–2024), where he portrayed Bingo Mariano. The series gave him a more extensive dramatic role and further strengthened his position as a leading actor.

His later projects include GG: Good Game and Tayo sa Wakas. He has also continued appearing in variety and entertainment programs, including ASAP.

Outside acting, Pangilinan enjoys sports and physical activities, including basketball, surfing, and running. He has also pursued music and recording projects.

His career represents the rise of a new generation of Filipino leading men who combine traditional television acting with streaming platforms, social media popularity, and entertainment hosting.
