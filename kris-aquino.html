About Kris Aquino

Kris Aquino, whose full name is Kristina Bernadette Cojuangco Aquino, is a Filipino television host, actress, producer, and public personality. Born on February 14, 1971, in Quezon City, she is the youngest daughter of former Philippine President Corazon Aquino and former Senator Benigno "Ninoy" Aquino Jr.

Aquino entered show business while still young and gradually became one of the country’s most recognizable television personalities. Her career developed through talk shows, entertainment programs, game shows, and film appearances. She became especially popular for her outspoken personality, interviewing style, and ability to connect with audiences.

Her horror-film breakthrough came with Feng Shui (2004), directed by Chito S. Roño. She portrayed Joy Ramirez, a woman whose discovery of a mysterious bagua mirror brings prosperity but also a terrifying curse. The movie became a major box-office success and established her as one of Philippine cinema’s most recognizable horror actresses.

She followed Feng Shui with Sukob (2006), another successful supernatural horror film directed by Roño. Her later horror and thriller projects included Dalaw (2010), Segunda Mano (2011), and Feng Shui 2 (2014). Her performances helped make these films major attractions during the Metro Manila Film Festival and reinforced her association with Filipino supernatural horror.

Beyond horror, Aquino appeared in films such as Mano Po, So... Happy Together, Sisterakas, Etiquette for Mistresses, and Crazy Rich Asians, in which she played Princess Intan. She also served as a producer and executive producer on several projects.

Her contribution to Philippine horror is particularly associated with Feng Shui and Sukob. Her distinctive screen personality, combined with her willingness to take on supernatural stories, helped make her a defining figure in the genre.
